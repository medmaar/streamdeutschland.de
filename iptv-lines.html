IPTV Lines – what they are and how to get a stable one

An IPTV line is your access credential to an IPTV service — a username, password and server URL. Getting the right line from a stable provider makes the difference between reliable 4K streaming and constant buffering. Here is everything you need to know.

What are IPTV lines?

IPTV lines are access credentials for an IPTV service. They typically come in two formats:

  • Xtream Codes line: a server URL, username and password — the most common format, supported by TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro and most modern apps.
  • M3U URL: a single playlist link that loads all channels at once — simpler, works with VLC and older apps.

Both formats give you access to the same channels. Xtream Codes lines unlock more features in apps like TiviMate, including catch-up TV, EPG and on-demand navigation.

Server stability

The quality of an IPTV line depends entirely on the server it connects to. A line from a provider with overloaded servers will buffer regardless of your internet speed. StreamDeutschland maintains redundant servers in Europe with 99.5% uptime — the line stays active even during peak hours.

Unlimited simultaneous connections (per plan)

Lines are sold by the number of simultaneous streams. A 1-device line covers one screen at a time; a 5-device line lets five screens stream at once. Choose based on how many devices you use simultaneously.
