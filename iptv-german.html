German IPTV – all German channels in one subscription

German IPTV gives you access to every major German TV channel over the internet — whether you live in Germany or abroad. ARD, ZDF, RTL, Bundesliga, 4K streams and 50,000+ channels in one subscription from StreamDeutschland.

What is German IPTV?

German IPTV delivers German television channels over your internet connection instead of satellite or cable. You get the same channels — ARD, ZDF, all regional public broadcasters, every major private channel — but streamed directly to any device: Fire TV, Smart TV, Android, iPhone or PC. No dish, no cable contract, no hardware installation.

Who uses German IPTV?

Two main groups: Germans living in Germany who want to replace or supplement cable, and Germans living abroad who want access to German-language TV. StreamDeutschland serves both — the server infrastructure is optimised for connections within Germany and across Europe.

German TV channels included

Public broadcasters (ARD / ZDF)

Every ARD and ZDF channel is included: Das Erste, ZDF, ZDFneo, ZDFinfo, 3sat, Arte, PHOENIX, KiKA, ONE, plus all nine regional ARD stations — NDR, WDR, BR, MDR, SWR, HR, RBB, Radio Bremen and SR. ORF 1 and ORF 2 from Austria and SRF 1 and SRF 2 from Switzerland are also part of the lineup.

Private German channels

Full coverage of all major private channels: RTL, RTL2, VOX, n-tv, NITRO, Super RTL, Sat.1, Pro7, Kabel1, Sixx, TELE 5, ProSieben MAXX, Sat.1 Gold, Comedy Central Germany, MTV Germany and more.

German sports channels

Sport1, Sky Sport channels, DAZN content, Eurosport 1 and 2 — plus all the live Bundesliga, Champions League and international sports coverage you would normally pay separately for.

German IPTV from abroad

If you are outside Germany and want to watch German TV, IPTV is the most practical solution. You get the full German channel lineup without a VPN and without geographical restrictions, since the streams are delivered directly to your device via your subscription credentials. Works in Austria, Switzerland, and across Europe and worldwide.

Setup in under ten minutes

After subscribing, you receive login credentials that work in TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, GSE Smart IPTV or any other standard IPTV app. Enter the three details (URL, username, password), confirm, and all channels load automatically. Our support team walks you through it via WhatsApp if needed.

Start with a free trial to verify the German channels run smoothly on your device before committing to a subscription.

Häufige Fragen

Can I watch German IPTV from outside Germany?

Yes. StreamDeutschland delivers German channels to subscribers worldwide without geographical restrictions. No VPN required.

Which German channels are included?

All ARD and ZDF channels including regional stations, all major private channels (RTL, Sat.1, Pro7, etc.), ORF and SRF, plus sports channels including Bundesliga coverage.

Is there a free trial for German IPTV?

Yes — request a free trial with no payment details required. You get full access to verify German channel quality before subscribing.

Does German IPTV work on Smart TVs?

Yes — on Samsung and LG Smart TVs via the Smart IPTV app, and on Android TV directly with TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro.

How much does German IPTV cost?

Subscriptions start at €9 for one month on one device. Multi-device and longer plans reduce the monthly cost to under €4.08.
