IPTV Subscription 2026 – plans, pricing and what's included

An IPTV subscription from StreamDeutschland gives you access to 50,000+ live channels, 300,000+ on-demand titles and stable 4K streams — all without a long-term contract. Here's everything you need to know before subscribing.

What's included in an IPTV subscription

Every StreamDeutschland subscription includes the full channel lineup from day one — there are no entry-level packages with restricted channels. What you get:

  • 50,000+ live channels including all major German broadcasters
  • 300,000+ movies and series on demand
  • 4K Ultra HD where available — native quality, not upscaled
  • Electronic Programme Guide (EPG) for all major channels
  • Support for all popular IPTV apps: TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, GSE Smart IPTV
  • Works on Fire TV, Android, Smart TV, iPhone, Apple TV and Windows

Subscription plans and pricing

Plans are structured by duration and number of simultaneous devices. Longer subscriptions cost less per month:

  • 1 month, 1 device – €9
  • 3 months, 1 device – €29 (€9.67/month)
  • 6 months, 1 device – €39 (€6.50/month)
  • 12 months, 1 device – €49 (€4.08/month)

Multi-device plans (2–5 simultaneous connections) are also available. Full pricing on the pricing page.

No auto-renewal — ever

Every subscription ends exactly when you expect it to. There is no automatic renewal, no need to cancel, and no surprise charges. If you want to continue, you actively choose a new plan. This applies to every duration — monthly, quarterly, six months or annual.

Free trial before subscribing

Before committing to any subscription, you can request a free trial. No payment details required. The trial gives you full access to the same channel lineup as a paid subscription, so you can verify stream quality on your own device and connection. Request your free trial here.

How to subscribe

Fill in the request form or contact us directly on WhatsApp. You'll receive your login credentials — username, password and server URL — within minutes. Setup in your chosen IPTV app takes under ten minutes, and our team helps you through it if needed.

Häufige Fragen

How much does an IPTV subscription cost?

Subscriptions start at €9 for one month on one device. The best value is the 12-month plan at €49 (€4.08/month). Multi-device plans are also available.

Does the subscription renew automatically?

No. Every subscription ends after the chosen period with no automatic renewal and no cancellation required.

Can I try before subscribing?

Yes — a free trial is available with no payment details required. You get full access to the same lineup as a paid subscription.

How quickly do I receive my subscription credentials?

Login details are usually delivered within minutes via WhatsApp or email after you complete the request.

How many devices can I use simultaneously?

Plans are available for 1 to 5 simultaneous devices. Choose the number that fits your household when selecting your plan.
