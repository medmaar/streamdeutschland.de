What is IPTV?

What is IPTV? Internet Protocol Television explained

IPTV — Internet Protocol Television — delivers TV channels over the internet instead of a satellite dish or cable connection. It works on any device with an internet connection: your Smart TV, Fire Stick, phone or computer. Here is how it works and whether it is right for you.

How IPTV works

Traditional television broadcasts a signal over satellite or through physical cable infrastructure. IPTV encodes the same TV content into digital data packets and sends them over the internet to your device in real time. Your IPTV app (TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, etc.) receives and decodes these packets and plays them on screen.

Because IPTV travels over the internet, it works on any device with an internet connection and an IPTV app — no dish, no cable box, no dedicated hardware required.

IPTV vs cable vs satellite

Cable TV

Cable requires a physical connection to the cable network, a set-top box and a monthly subscription tied to a specific address. Channels are predetermined by the cable operator. Adding sport or premium channels costs extra.

Satellite TV

Satellite requires a dish, a receiver and a clear line of sight to the satellite. Setup is more complex and the hardware is fixed. Moving or renting means potential installation problems.

IPTV

IPTV needs only an internet connection and a compatible app. No dish, no cable, no address restriction. Works anywhere with sufficient bandwidth — in Germany, abroad and while travelling. Channels can include everything cable or satellite offers, plus international content and on-demand libraries.

What you need for IPTV

  • Internet connection: at least 25 Mbit/s for HD, 50 Mbit/s for 4K
  • A compatible device: Fire TV Stick, Smart TV, Android box, iPhone, PC or Apple TV
  • An IPTV app: TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro or GSE Smart IPTV (all free or low cost)
  • An IPTV subscription: login credentials from a provider like StreamDeutschland

Is IPTV legal?

IPTV technology is completely legal. The legality of specific content depends on whether the provider holds the appropriate broadcast licences for the channels it carries. Häufige Fragen

What does IPTV stand for?

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television — television delivered over the internet rather than through cable or satellite.

Do I need special hardware for IPTV?

No dedicated hardware is required. IPTV works on devices you likely already own: a Fire TV Stick, Smart TV, Android phone or tablet, iPhone or Windows PC.

Is IPTV legal?

IPTV technology is legal. Whether a specific IPTV service is legal depends on whether it holds the appropriate broadcast licences. Always use a provider that operates transparently.

How fast does my internet need to be for IPTV?

At least 25 Mbit/s download for HD streams, and 50 Mbit/s or more for stable 4K. Most modern broadband connections in Germany easily exceed this.

What is the difference between IPTV and streaming services like Netflix?

Netflix is a VOD (video on demand) service — you choose what to watch, when. IPTV delivers live television channels in real time, like traditional TV, but also includes on-demand libraries.
